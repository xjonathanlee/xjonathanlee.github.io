River Runner

INFO

In 1968 Venturi, Brown and Izenour took a studio of Yale students to the Las Vegas Strip. Their findings titled "Learning from Las Vegas" has become a seminal work in Architecture and Product design as a source of record. I am seeking to approach equestrian culture in the same way, stable masters speak for themselves doing away with the "Horse Stereotype". Each Vignette analyzes how their world actually works.

CONTEXT

This documentary series follows five equestrian trainers, moving past the “horse people” stereotype to offer a contemporary look at equine management and culture. Each film is titled “On” a single emotion or quality, such as tenacity, trust or perseverance.
