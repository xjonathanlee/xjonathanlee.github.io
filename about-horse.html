About

Jonathan Lee is a designer and communicator based in Richmond, BC, working between data, live events and moving image.

I design interfaces, tell stories with data, and make things people can walk into. River Runners is my first long-form documentary.

Practice

  • UX & Interface Design

    Research-led design for tools that make complex information easier to explore.

  • Data Visualization

    Turning datasets into interactive, readable stories for public audiences.

  • Video & Photography

    Documentary, event and portrait work, from shooting through grading and edit.

  • Event Production

    Producing and running events and installations under OFFLINE and Anikura.

River Runners

River Runners is a portrait of equine trainers and stable masters, told through five vignettes. It asks what matters in the Hunter and Jumper disciplines, and invites a new perspective on equestrian training and culture.
