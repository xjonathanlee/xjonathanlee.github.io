    What Goes Around Comes Around

    We were invited with another Swiss agency to celebrate the 75th anniversary of the union between Swiss and Italian graphic design. These two different realities had met many years ago and had all these possibilities to develop new languages, ​​new styles, on very important projects. So we tried to recreate the spirit of their collaboration.

    ― Jekyll & Hyde

    with Bureau Collective for polyedra (2011)

    Studio Practices

    People would expect us, for our 20th anniversary, to show off past works we were proud of. We decided instead to create something new, more beautiful than any previous project. We did not want to celebrate the past, so the theme we decided to develop was "the future."

    ― Jekyll & Hyde

    Un Sedicesimo (2017)

    Studio Practices

    Yes, in fact, we often define ourselves as artisans - we are not artists. "Craftsmen" means that we really like to be hands on with the projects we develop. Our agency stayed small for this reason, because we like to work on everything.

    ― Jekyll & Hyde

    Un Sedicesimo (2017)

    Studio Practices

    Another aspect of craftsmanship that matches with our vision is working and doing well every day, studying small particulars that become a unique source of communication which then becomes what our work is made of.
